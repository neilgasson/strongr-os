## Release scheduling rules

- One approved primary daily experience may be highlighted each relative day; no draft content crosses into the app.
- Every day receives a rotating Scripture reference and a governed public editorial journal-or-prayer prompt requirement.
- A completed item remains unavailable until its individual rights, review, exact-version approval, package, and deliberate delivery gate are complete.
- No schedule row authorizes automatic publication, audio generation, uploading, or a subscriber entitlement change.
- Seasonal reordering requires an owner decision after launch-date selection; the 56-day count and review gates remain fixed.
