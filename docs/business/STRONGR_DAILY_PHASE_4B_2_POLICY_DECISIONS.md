# Strongr Daily Phase 4B.2 content-policy decisions

**Status:** Owner approved; governs Phase 4B.3 proposals

**Recorded:** 2026-07-31

**Activation authorized by this record:** None

**Provider use or spending authorized by this record:** None

## Approved direction

1. One rights-cleared golden example is sufficient for an initial profile
   activation proposal. Two or more examples are preferred before scaled
   production.
2. Private user prayer and journal responses are completely excluded from
   provider workflows. Public editorial prayers and prompts are separate
   governed outputs.
3. Scripture-series commentary remains concise and subordinate to the passage,
   whether full text is rights-cleared or supplied through an authorized link.
4. A series manifest defines its audience, overall pastoral goal, and
   repetition controls in addition to its sequence.
5. Owner-created STRONGR content may use a simplified rights record confirming
   ownership or control and exact approved uses. Third-party and Scripture
   sources require full rights records.
6. The first-reserve primary profile set is limited to:
   `guided_audio_reflection`, `devotional_experience`, and
   `scripture_reading_series`.
7. Prayer prompts, personal takeaways, descriptions, artwork prompts, social
   captions, and metadata remain supporting outputs within a primary profile.
   They do not activate as independent profiles.

## Existing Strongr Daily standards preserved

- Christ-centred and Scripture-rooted.
- Warm, peaceful, welcoming, reverent, premium, modern, and accessible.
- No prosperity gospel, guaranteed outcomes, or claims that AI speaks for God.
- Scripture translation and quotation rights fail closed.
- Human Scripture, theological/pastoral, and editorial review remain required.
- Exact-version approval remains AAL2 protected.
- Nothing is automatically narrated, uploaded, published, or released.

## Phase 4B.3 application

Phase 4B.3 may prepare one inactive, checksum-bound activation proposal for
`guided_audio_reflection`. It may not activate that proposal, call a provider,
generate audio, publish content, or modify production. A separate exact owner
approval is required after the proposal, source manifest, rights record, and
checksums are complete.
