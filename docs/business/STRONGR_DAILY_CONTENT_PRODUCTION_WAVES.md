## Cross-wave production controls

- A content item may move only forward through the governed lifecycle; reviews and approval target an exact immutable version.
- No profile activation, provider call, translation, narration, audio, upload, or release is authorized by the wave assignment.
- Public editorial prayer is distinct from private My Prayers. Private prayer, journal, mood, care, and personal-response text are excluded from production inputs.
- Source references and existing title/asset families are not a license to quote, adapt, narrate, or distribute raw source prose.
- Each output needs a traceable rights record, reviewed metadata, and an explicit owner decision before delivery to Strongr Daily.
