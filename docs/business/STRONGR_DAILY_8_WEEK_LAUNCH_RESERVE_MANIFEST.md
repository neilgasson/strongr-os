## Purpose and hard boundaries

This is the authoritative planning record for the first eight-week Strongr Daily reserve. It schedules production work; it does not create a script, narration, audio file, translated copy, provider request, or publishable item.

- No OpenAI, ElevenLabs, translation, analytics, upload, or publishing call was made to create this manifest.
- No raw Quiet Trust prose, private prayer, journal entry, care disclosure, or full Scripture text is included.
- Scripture references are planning metadata only. Each production item needs translation-specific source, quotation, narration, and distribution rights verification before use.
- Every planned public item remains subject to the governed lifecycle: brief, draft, automated checks, Scripture review, theological review, editorial review, exact-version approval, immutable package, and controlled delivery.
- The existing Strongr Daily product remains the approved customer experience reference. This manifest does not redesign it or alter production systems.

The owner-controlled private canonical library establishes that named materials, approved exports, continuity guides, and media exist, but it does **not** blanket-authorize derivative use, provider use, quotation, publication, or narration. “Existing reference” in this manifest means a known title, package, or asset family may inform production planning only. Primary release-item entitlement is planned as **16 Free** and **40 Premium**. Premium remains focused on library depth: the full journeys, complete studies, all guided-prayer and Scripture-reading libraries as configured for launch, Sleep & Rest, full transcripts, continuous play, later downloads, and approved multilingual audio. Final paywall placement needs entitlement/product review before release.
